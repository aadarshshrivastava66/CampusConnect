Cricket Tournament Notice

All students are informed that inter-college cricket team selection trials will begin from 12 Jun 2026 at the college sports ground.

Students interested in participating must register their names in the sports department before 10 jun 2026.

Trails start from 10th may.

Players should carry their college ID card and sports kit during trials.

Selected players will represent the college in the zonal cricket tournament.

For more details contact the sports officer.
